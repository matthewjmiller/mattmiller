# Against AI

![Against AI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/matthewjmiller/mattmiller/refs/heads/gh-pages/_assets/writers%2Bagainst%2BAI%2Blogos_WRITERS%2BAGAINST%2BAI%2B1.webp)

I am a [Writer Against AI](https://www.writersagainstai.net/) or, one might say, a writer for humans. Here is some of my writing explicitly concerned with resisting digital technology.

["Why I Am Not Going to Use AI"](https://buttondown.com/matthewjmiller/archive/why-i-am-not-going-to-use-ai/) - statement of principles.

["Composition as the Art of Loading Brush"](https://www.frontporchrepublic.com/2023/05/composition-as-the-art-of-loading-brush/) - why teaching writing must include deliberate care, not algorithmic speed.

["Empty Words: Against Artificial Language"](https://mereorthodoxy.com/empty-words-against-artificial-language) - why automatically generated writing pollutes our language.

["On Lower Thinking"](https://buttondown.com/matthewjmiller/archive/on-lower-thinking/) - why there's no such thing as "mechanical cognition."

["The Center for Needless Splendor"](https://buttondown.com/matthewjmiller/archive/new-initiatives-at-the-center-for/) - against pragmatism and for heedless joy.
